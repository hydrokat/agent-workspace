# Business Flows

Process flows, decision paths, and operational context for domain workflows.

## Contents

Each business flow gets its own file or subdirectory:

- `user-registration.md` — Sign-up, verification, onboarding flow
- `order-fulfillment.md` — Cart, checkout, payment, shipping lifecycle
- `billing-cycle.md` — Invoice generation, payment collection, dunning
- `notification-routing.md` — Email, push, SMS trigger conditions

# Sales Enablement

Field-ready sales enablement playbook, adapted from [coreyhaines31/marketingskills (sales-enablement)](https://skillsmp.com/creators/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/skills-sales-enablement). Equips founders, account executives, and solution engineers with crisp discovery scripts, honest competitive battlecards, objection responses, and ROI framing.

## When To Use

- Preparing a sales team or founder for outbound/inbound buyer conversations.
- Creating competitive battlecards against named incumbents or DIY status-quo alternatives.
- Building an objection-handling matrix, discovery call guide, ROI calculator model, or champion one-pager.
- Invoked directly via `/sales-enablement`.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Map The Buying Committee & Deal Motion

Identify who participates in the deal and what blocks each stakeholder:
- **Champion / Practitioner**: Needs faster workflow, lower friction, and quick internal credibility.
- **Economic Buyer**: Needs measurable ROI, payback period, and low implementation risk.
- **Technical / Security Reviewer**: Needs architecture clarity, data boundaries, auth/permissions, and compliance answers.
- **Trigger & Deal Stage**: Discovery, technical evaluation, security review, or procurement close.

### Step 2: Build Discovery Call Scripts

Structure discovery around diagnosing cost of inaction before pitching features:
1. **Context & Trigger**: "What prompted looking at this right now vs. six months ago?"
2. **Current Workflow & Pain**: "Walk me through how the team handles this today and where it breaks."
3. **Quantified Impact**: "How many hours, missed opportunities, or incidents does that cost per week/month?"
4. **Decision Criteria & Process**: "What has to be true for you to call a 30-day pilot a win, and who signs off?"

### Step 3: Craft Honest Competitive Battlecards

Compare against both **direct competitors** and **status quo (DIY / spreadsheets / manual scripts)**:
- **Where We Win**: Concrete architectural or workflow advantages backed by verifiable proof.
- **Where Competitor Wins**: Acknowledge honestly so reps never get blindsided on a call; pair with how we mitigate or reframe.
- **Landmines To Plant**: Fair, high-signal questions the buyer can ask the competitor during evaluation (e.g., hidden setup time, lock-in, or per-seat cost explosions).
- **Disqualification Signals**: Signs a prospect is a poor fit so reps protect pipeline quality.

### Step 4: Construct The Objection Handling Matrix

Address recurring objections with **Acknowledge -> Reframe -> Proof -> Next Question**:
- **Price / Budget**: Anchor against the quantified cost of the status quo and payback window.
- **Timing / Bandwidth**: Propose a bounded low-lift pilot or migration checklist.
- **Build vs. Buy**: Break down ongoing maintenance, edge-case debt, and opportunity cost.
- **Security / Risk**: Provide concrete architecture facts, deployment boundaries, and verification paths.

### Step 5: Build ROI Framing & Champion One-Pager

Give the champion a concise internal business case they can forward to their leadership:
- **Before vs. After**: Current baseline hours/cost vs. projected post-adoption metrics.
- **Conservative ROI Formula**: Explicit inputs (`team_size * hours_saved * loaded_rate - annual_cost`) with conservative assumptions marked clearly.
- **Low-Risk Pilot Plan**: 14–30 day proof-of-value milestones and exit criteria.

### Step 6: Package The Kit

Fill `assets/sales-enablement.md` with battlecards, discovery questions, objection matrix, ROI calculator inputs, and the champion one-pager.

## Mandates

- **Trigger**: Invoke with `/sales-enablement`.
- **Honest Differentiation**: Never fabricate competitor weaknesses or unverified benchmarks; mark assumptions explicitly.
- **Buyer-Language First**: Write talk tracks in plain, conversational language reps can actually say out loud.
- **Champion-Ready**: Include a forwardable executive summary so the buyer can sell internally without you in the room.

# Offer Engineering

High-converting offer design framework, adapted from [coreyhaines31/marketingskills (offers)](https://skillsmp.com/creators/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/skills-offers). Transforms a raw product or service into an irresistible, low-friction offer using the value equation, strategic bonuses, risk reversal, and ethical conversion triggers.

## When To Use

- Improving low landing-page or sales-call conversion without resorting to heavy discounting.
- Packaging a launch offer, founding-member tier, or high-ticket onboarding package.
- Designing a lead magnet or free tool that bridges naturally into the core paid product.
- Invoked directly via `/offers`.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Audit Against The Value Equation

Evaluate and strengthen all four levers of perceived value:
1. **Dream Outcome (Maximize)**: State the specific, tangible end state the buyer actually wants — not software features.
2. **Perceived Likelihood of Achievement (Maximize)**: Back claims with proof (case studies, benchmarks, live sandbox, walkthroughs, transparent methodology).
3. **Time Delay (Minimize)**: Compress time-to-first-value (done-with-you setup, starter templates, instant import, quick-win milestone in < 15 minutes).
4. **Effort & Sacrifice (Minimize)**: Remove migration pain, steep learning curves, and hidden implementation chores.

### Step 2: Build The Core Offer + Bonus Stack

Bundle high-value, low-marginal-cost accelerators that directly eliminate buyer obstacles:
- **Core Deliverable**: The main product/service and its exact scope.
- **Obstacle-Killer Bonuses**: Each bonus must solve a specific next objection (e.g., "Done-for-you migration checklist", "Starter template pack", "30-minute architecture onboarding"). Never pad with irrelevant fluff.
- **Anchor Value**: Show standalone value honestly alongside the bundled offer price.

### Step 3: Engineer Risk Reversal (Guarantees)

Shift perceived risk from the buyer back onto you where appropriate:
- **Unconditional Guarantee**: Time-boxed "no-questions-asked" refund (best for self-serve / low-touch).
- **Conditional / Outcome Guarantee**: "Achieve [milestone] in [X] days if you complete [onboarding steps], or we extend/refund/work free until you do" (best for B2B/services).
- **Try-Before-Commit / Sandbox**: Free diagnostic, pilot, or reverse trial that demonstrates value before payment.

### Step 4: Apply Ethical Urgency & Scarcity

Use only **real, verifiable** reasons to act now — never fake countdown timers:
- **Cohort / Onboarding Capacity**: Limited weekly implementation slots due to real human support bandwidth.
- **Seasonal / Launch Window**: Founding pricing or launch bonus expiring on a true date when the cohort closes.
- **Cost of Delay**: Tangible deadline the buyer already faces (compliance cutoff, Q4 budget lock, upcoming launch).

### Step 5: Design The Lead-Magnet-To-Core-Offer Bridge

Pair the core offer with a front-end entry point (audit, calculator, template, mini-course, or free diagnostic) that solves one narrow problem immediately and naturally reveals the larger problem your core offer solves.

### Step 6: Document The Offer

Fill `assets/offer-design.md` with the value-equation breakdown, bonus stack, guarantee copy, ethical urgency rules, and landing-page offer block.

## Mandates

- **Trigger**: Invoke with `/offers`.
- **Ethical Conversion Only**: Never recommend fake scarcity, deceptive countdown timers, or unfulfillable guarantees.
- **Bonuses Solve Objections**: Every bonus must address a concrete friction point (setup, speed, migration, confidence).
- **Concrete Copy**: Include ready-to-use headline, offer-stack bullets, guarantee wording, and CTA text.

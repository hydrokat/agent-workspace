# Social Media Strategy & Content

High-engagement social distribution framework, adapted from [coreyhaines31/marketingskills (social)](https://skillsmp.com/creators/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/skills-social). Builds repeatable content pillars, scroll-stopping hooks, platform-native adaptations, and conversion-aware distribution loops.

## When To Use

- Planning a product launch social blitz or ongoing organic founder/brand presence.
- Turning technical launches, case studies, or product insights into X threads, LinkedIn posts, and short-form visual/video scripts.
- Designing a weekly publishing cadence and engagement workflow.
- Invoked directly via `/social`.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Define Audience, Voice & Content Pillars

Anchor every post in 3–4 repeatable **Content Pillars** so the account builds authority instead of random noise:
1. **Problem / Contrarian Insight**: Challenge a broken industry default or hidden bottleneck.
2. **Build-In-Public / Behind-The-Scenes**: Architecture decisions, benchmarks, ship logs, real trade-offs.
3. **Playbooks & How-Tos**: Step-by-step workflows, templates, or teardowns the ICP can apply immediately.
4. **Customer Proof & Outcomes**: Before/after metrics, mini case studies, user wins.

Define voice boundaries: sharp, specific, zero corporate buzzwords, concrete numbers/examples over vague hype.

### Step 2: Apply Proven Hook Frameworks

Treat the first 1–2 lines (or first 3 seconds) as 80% of reach:
- **Outcome + Constraint**: "We cut CI wait times from 18m to 3m without changing our runner tier."
- **Contrarian Mistake**: "Most teams think pricing fails on price. It almost always fails on the value metric."
- **Specific Teardown**: "I audited 12 developer onboarding flows. Here are the 4 friction patterns killing activation:"
- **Template / Artifact Offer**: "Here is the exact checklist we use before shipping a release:"

### Step 3: Adapt Natively By Platform

Never copy-paste identical text across platforms; tailor structure and norms:
- **X / Twitter**: Punchy single posts or 4–7 post threads; visual/code snippet/diagram in post #1; link in reply or final post; conversational replies over broadcast-only links.
- **LinkedIn**: Strong 2-line hook before the `...see more` fold; short scannable paragraphs; narrative or carousel/document asset; genuine question at the end to spark domain comments.
- **Short-Form Video / Visual Demos (YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok)**: 3-second visual hook showing the end result first, 20–45s tight demo/explanation, crisp captions, one clear next step.

### Step 4: Engineer Engagement & Conversion Loops

- **Zero-Click Value First**: Deliver the core insight inside the feed so readers reward the post with saves/reposts; use soft CTAs ("Full template in replies" / "Try it free") rather than naked link spam.
- **Distribution Ritual**: Pre-brief partners/team, reply substantively in the first 30–60 minutes, repurpose top 10% performers across formats 30–60 days later.
- **Measurement**: Track saves, meaningful replies, profile-to-site clicks, and qualified signups (not raw impressions alone).

### Step 5: Deliver The Campaign Playbook

Fill `assets/social-campaign.md` with content pillars, weekly cadence, ready-to-publish platform drafts, visual/media prompts, and tracking rules.

## Mandates

- **Trigger**: Invoke with `/social`.
- **Platform-Native Copy**: Produce distinct drafts tailored to each target platform's format and fold limits.
- **Specificity Beats Hype**: Replace generic adjectives ("revolutionary", "game-changing") with concrete mechanics, numbers, or examples.
- **No Unverified Claims**: Every metric or capability cited in drafts must come from verified project facts or explicitly marked placeholders.
